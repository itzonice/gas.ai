# Legal rules for StudyPulse (September 2026)

What a small, AI-built study app has to follow to avoid lawsuits, regulator fines, and
store rejection, and where StudyPulse stands on each. Researched September 26, 2026.
It's general information, not legal advice. Payments and students' data make a one-time
lawyer review of the privacy policy and terms worth it before launch.

Status: ✅ done in code · 🟡 partly · 👤 needs the owner · ➖ not applicable.

## 2. Fixed in this pass (September 26, 2026)

1. **AI consent before anything is sent to Anthropic.** A prompt names Anthropic, lists
   what's sent and not sent, says the AI can be wrong, and asks "Allow and continue".
   The database refuses syllabus parses and card generation without consent (errcode
   `SPA15`), so an old app version or a direct API call can't skip it. Withdraw anytime
   in Settings (web and mobile). Every choice is logged with the policy version.
   Existing accounts start with it off, so everyone sees the prompt once.
2. **Privacy policy:** a "How we use AI" section and a "Legal bases" section for the
   EU/UK (contract, consent, legitimate interests, legal obligation; transfers).
3. **Public pages:** `/delete-account` (Google Play requirement, works signed out) and an
   `/accessibility` statement with a contact, both in the footer.
4. **Written security program and breach runbook** (L9), with a retention schedule.

## 3. What only the owner can do

1. Fill every `[bracket]` in the legal pages and these docs; set the company variables.
2. Lawyer review of the privacy policy, terms, and refund policy (payments are in v1).
3. Sign data processing agreements with each processor (most are click-through in the
   dashboards) and tick them off in [security-program.md](security-program.md).
4. Register a DMCA agent; do the trademark search; replace template icons.
5. Decide the approach for the state app-store age laws before the mobile launch.
6. App Store privacy label and Play Data safety form (answers drafted in
   [data-inventory.md](data-inventory.md); add "shared with a third-party AI provider").
7. VoiceOver and TalkBack passes on real phones.
